## How to use this

Pick a risk, do its "suggested test" as a contribution (several map to tickets M3/M10/S1/S5/S6), and update Confidence with what you learn. A risk register you *personally* moved from hypothesis to confirmed/refuted is a first-class interview artifact — it demonstrates evidence discipline, which is rarer than knowledge.
